The red-boxed vehicle is stationary.

Judge ONLY whether this vehicle clearly occupies TWO OR MORE PAINTED parking bays.

YES:
A painted parking-bay separator clearly passes under or through the vehicle's parking footprint, or the vehicle clearly occupies substantial areas on both sides of a separator between two marked bays.

NO:
The vehicle clearly belongs to one marked bay, even if it touches a line, slightly crosses a boundary, has a small bumper overhang, or is mildly angled.

Also answer NO if the vehicle is simply parked in an unmarked roadside or open-pavement area. That is handled by another question.

UNCERTAIN:
The relevant separator or parking lines cannot be seen well enough to decide.

IMPORTANT:
A tiny tire touch or minor line crossing is NOT enough for YES.
YES requires clear substantial occupancy of multiple marked bays.
